PASS if the answer does not just pick one: it frames the two options as mutually exclusive choices and works through what would have to be true for each to be a good choice (conditions on customers, competitors, capabilities, economics), keeps only the conditions that are truly binding, and proposes testing the condition judged least likely first, ideally designed with the most skeptical person on the team.
FAIL if it recommends one option from general market knowledge, or gives a generic pros-and-cons table without conditions that would have to be true and how to test them.
