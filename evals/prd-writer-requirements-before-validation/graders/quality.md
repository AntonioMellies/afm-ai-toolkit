PASS if the answer does not simply produce a long requirements and user-story document: it points out that requirements and stories should follow validation of the idea (value, usability, feasibility, business viability), asks for the objective/key results and customer evidence the module serves, and proposes a lighter path (opportunity assessment first, then a validated prototype as the specification, complemented by business rules and acceptance criteria where needed).
FAIL if it just writes a complete traditional PRD with functional requirements and user stories without questioning the lack of validation or asking about objectives and evidence.
