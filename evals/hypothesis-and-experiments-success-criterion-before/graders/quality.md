PASS if the answer insists on defining the success criterion and the decision BEFORE running the test, expresses the criterion as an absolute count out of the 10 people (for example 'at least N of 10 do X'), not only as a percentage, and asks or states what will be done for each outcome (go, adjust, stop).
FAIL if it plans the test without a success criterion, leaves the criterion to be judged afterwards, or sets the criterion only as a percentage such as '70% liked it'.
